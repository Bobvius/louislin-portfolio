Project Overview

Designed an end-to-end electromechanical bionic hand capable of real-time spatial tracking. The system captures 21 hand landmarks via a Python/MediaPipe vision pipeline and streams coordinate data over UART to an ESP32-S3 microcontroller executing real-time PWM actuation.

Figure 1: Custom 2-layer PCB layout rendered in KiCad with isolated power plane routing.

Power Electronics & Protection

To prevent motor inductive spikes from resetting the ESP32 logic, I designed dedicated power regulation and input protection directly onto the PCB:

  • AP63203 Buck Regulator: Stepped down 12V DC input to a stable 5V rail for 6 high-torque servos.
  • ESD & Reverse Protection: Integrated USBLC6-2SC6 diodes on native USB-C lines and 5.1kΩ CC pull-downs for standard PD configuration.
Figure 2: Power supply schematic featuring step-down regulation and decoupling capacitors.

Firmware & Testing

Developed low-level C++ firmware utilizing FreeRTOS tasks to parse incoming coordinate payloads via I²C master-slave communication and compute real-time duty cycle adjustments under sub-millisecond latency.
